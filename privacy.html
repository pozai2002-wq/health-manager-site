Privacy Policy

Health Manager is a single-user personal app. It reads only the owner's own Google Health data, using read-only scopes, for private analysis of fitness, sleep, and health information.

Data is stored privately on the owner's own machine. It is never sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for ads.

The owner can revoke access anytime at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
